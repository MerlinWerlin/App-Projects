Top 10 takeaways

  1. Leaders win with cute/anime templates + lessons + own-photo tracing: we now have a Cute & chibi category; a beginner course is queued.
  2. Paywalls are the #1 complaint in almost every app: 'every tool free, no ads, no subscription' is our headline.
  3. Typical cost is US$30-40/yr (A$50-60), weekly plans up to US$9.99+/wk: a one-time US$4.99 Plus is about 85% cheaper.
  4. Overlay drift / refocus is the #2 complaint: focus now stays put while the image is locked.
  5. Grid, mirror and favourites are almost absent from listings: we have them, so show them in screenshots.
  6. Time-lapse recording is common: ours never records the guide and has no watermark.
  7. Users lose work: new free 'My drawings' gallery keeps snapshots on the phone.
  8. Confusing first use and icon-only controls recur: new 4-step setup guide, captions everywhere.
  9. Store pages lead with the outcome: our first 3 shots = drawing on paper, 'free, no ads', photo to sketch.
  10. AU prices run 1.5-2x US in this category: A$7.99 one-time is a strong local message.

Proposed (owner decides): all tracing free forever; optional Linework Plus one-time US$4.99 / A$7.99 for extra template packs and themes; tip jar.
