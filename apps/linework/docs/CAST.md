# The Linework cast (fb-1022b B3)

The owner asked for "characters from stuff". Real film, TV, game, Disney, Marvel or anime characters and lookalikes are off-limits (App Review 5.2, owners enforce actively), so Linework has its own small recurring cast. All are original designs made in this repo; any one of them could become the app's mascot. Keep every appearance on-model (the traits below) so they read as the same character across categories. Their templates carry the tags `cast` and the lower-case name (e.g. `nova`); names use the character's name (e.g. "Nova plants a flag on the moon").

The team's ids use the `hero2-` prefix and the lower-case name as a tag (`starbright`, `volt`, `tidewave`, `acorn`, `gearbit`). Pip appears as a caped hero (`hero2-pip-hero`) and in the Game world platform level (`game2-platform-scene`).

Rules: never give the cast a costume, emblem, logo or silhouette of an existing character. No skulls-and-crossbones logos (use a shell or a star). Names above are generic words chosen to avoid known characters; do not add surnames or catchphrases.
