Linework Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 October 2026 · App: Linework (iOS and Android, com.jared797.linework)

✓
Data Not Collected Linework does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any data about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your pictures and drawings stay on your device.

What Linework does not do

  • No analytics, crash-reporting or advertising tools, and no tracking of any kind across apps or websites.
  • No sign-up, login or account. We never ask for your name, email or any other personal details.
  • No uploads. Photos you pick, photos you take, the outlines Linework makes from them and snapshots of your paper never leave your device.
  • No network calls of its own. Linework works fully offline. The only time it goes online is the standard app-update check described below.

Camera

Linework uses the camera to show the live view of your paper behind the picture you are tracing. The camera picture is shown on screen and processed only on your device. Linework records video only when you tap Record to make a timelapse, and the recording stays on your device. Linework does not use the microphone; timelapse clips have no sound. If you would rather not allow the camera, Lightbox mode lets you trace over the screen without it.

Photos

When you choose Trace a photo, you pick a picture with the system photo picker. Linework reads only the picture you choose, turns it into an outline on your device if you ask it to, and keeps a copy in its own private storage so it can show your recent pictures. It never uploads it.

Linework saves a timelapse to your Photos library only when you tap Save to Photos. That is the only time it writes to your library.

What is stored, and where

Everything Linework keeps is stored locally on your device, inside the app:

  • your settings (language, starting opacity, grid, haptics, keep-awake, focus lock),
  • your recent pictures and favourite templates,
  • drawings you save to My drawings, and your local drawing stats and streak,
  • whether you own Linework Plus, and when your free trial started.

None of this is sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes all of it. You can also remove single recent pictures or drawings in the app.

Free trial and the Linework Plus purchase

Linework is free to try for 14 days; the start date of the trial is kept only on your device. After that, tracing your own photos stays free, and Linework Plus is an optional one-time purchase that unlocks the full template library and outline mode. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own privacy policies. Linework never sees your payment details, name or account; it only learns from the store whether the purchase went through, so it can unlock Plus on your device and offer Restore purchases.

App updates

Store builds of Linework check for small app updates (new wording, fixes, templates) when they open, using the Expo EAS Update service. That request contains only technical information needed to deliver the right update, such as the app version, the platform and the update channel, plus a random identifier the update library creates on the device. It contains nothing about you, and none of your pictures, drawings or settings. We do not use it to identify or track you.

Children's privacy

Linework is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13 (or the equivalent age where you live). There are no ads, no chat, no social features and no links that let a child share anything. Purchases go through the App Store or Google Play, where parents can use the store's own parental controls such as Ask to Buy.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on a server. All your Linework data is on your device and under your control.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect. We will never start collecting data without saying so here and in the store listing first.
