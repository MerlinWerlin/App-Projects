# Linework: product

Inferred from the code, docs/PLAN.md and docs/MARKET.md during the Nov 2026 design overhaul; Jared can correct it.

- **What it is:** an iPhone app for tracing onto real paper. You pick a photo or one of the built-in line-art
  templates, Linework turns it into an outline, and the camera view shows that outline over your paper so you can
  draw over it. Saved drawings, timelapses and a workshop of short lessons sit around that loop.
- **Who uses it:** people who like to draw but want help with proportions: hobby sketchers, parents drawing
  with kids, people making cards or lettering, tattoo and mural beginners. Often with the phone on a stand,
  one hand holding a pencil, at a table.
- **The one job:** get from "I want to draw this" to tracing on paper in as few taps as possible. Tracing a photo
  is the main action and is always free.
- **Money:** Linework Plus (one-time purchase, optional 14-day trial) unlocks the full template library and
  photo-to-outline extras. Tracing is never gated.
- **Platform:** Expo / React Native, iPhone only, light and dark, 12 languages including RTL Arabic and CJK.
- **Status:** live on the App Store (1.0.3). Shipping is Jared's call.
- **Tone:** calm and practical, like a good art-supply shop: it knows paper and pencils, it doesn't shout.
