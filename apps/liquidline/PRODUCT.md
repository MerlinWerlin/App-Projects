# Product

## Platform
ios

## Users
Licensed air-conditioning and refrigeration technicians (fridgies, HVAC techs) on their own iPhone, on site: on a
roof, in a plant room or at the service van, often in gloves, in sun or a dark ceiling space, one hand on the gauges.

## Product Purpose
A service-van toolkit: pressure–temperature lookups, superheat and subcooling, extra charge for long line sets,
evacuation, line sets, a rough room load, flare torque and unit conversion, set up for the tech's country (units,
refrigerants, licence and standards names, stocked pair coils / line sets). Success: the right number in seconds,
with the working shown, and nothing that could be mistaken for the manufacturer's data.

## Brand
Gauge manifold colours: blue = low side, red = high side, amber = safety. Big condensed numerals (Barlow Condensed).
Name and icon: a split blue/red droplet on a copper liquid line.

## Constraints
- Expo / React Native, iPhone only, no OTA updates. StoreKit 2 module `modules/liquidline-store`.
- 7-day free trial (free non-consumable) + one-time Full Unlock; PT, chart, facts, converter and saved jobs always free.
- Data Not Collected; no account, no network except the App Store.
- Country data cited in docs/SOURCES.md; retailer and brand names never in the UI (tested).
- PT data generated by tools/gen_pt.py (CoolProp 8), tested against NIST and makers' tables.
