# Rootpass: Weld & Fab Calcs

iPhone calculator for welders, boilermakers and metal fabricators (Expo SDK 58, expo-router). Started 23 Nov 2026.

| Tool | Free? | Core | Source of the maths |
|---|---|---|---|
| Saddle cut (fishmouth / cope) + true-scale PDF | Full Unlock | `src/core/saddle.ts` | cylinder intersection, checked against a numeric solver in tests |
| Lobster-back bends + mitre wrap template | Full Unlock | `src/core/lobster.ts` | ASME B31.3 304.2.3 |
| Frame mitres (rectangles, polygons, any corner) | Free | `src/core/mitre.ts` | Machinery's Handbook |
| Cut list (fewest stock lengths, kerf, trim) | Full Unlock | `src/core/cutlist.ts` | exact branch and bound (Martello & Toth) |
| Steel weight | Free | `src/core/weight.ts` | EN 10219-2, AS/NZS 3679.1, EN 10088-1 |
| Weld metal / filler | Full Unlock | `src/core/weld.ts` | Lincoln Procedure Handbook |
| Sheet bending | Free | `src/core/bend.ts` | Machinery's Handbook, ASM 14B |
| Bolt circle (PDF drill template is Full Unlock) | Free | `src/core/pcd.ts` | Machinery's Handbook |

- PDFs: `src/core/pdf.ts` (dependency-free writer, 1 mm = 72/25.4 pt) and `src/core/templates.ts` (tiling over A4/Letter,
  100 mm ruler on every sheet). `src/core/templates.test.ts` reads the PDF back and checks the scale.
- Purchases: `com.jared797.rootpass.unlock` (A$4.99) and the free user-started `com.jared797.rootpass.trial7`
  ("7-day Trial"); flow adapted from Cue (`src/plus`).
- Tests: `npm test` (node:test, no RN). CI: `.github/workflows/rootpass-check.yml`.
- Screens: `.github/workflows/rootpass-capture.yml` builds a simulator app with `EXPO_PUBLIC_ROOTPASS_CAPTURE=1`
  (everything open, never in a store build) and captures every screen with Maestro (`store/capture/*.yaml`);
  `store/compose.py` turns captures into App Store screenshots.
- Store: `store/listing.json` (generated by `store/make_listing.py`), applied by `scripts/asc/asc.py`.
- Art: icon and illustrations are original vector art in `assets/src/*.svg` and `src/ui/Art.tsx`.
- App Store Connect: Apple ID 6838129148, SKU rootpass, Expo project @jared797/rootpass.
