Audience and job: someone on the sofa controlling one of several TVs one-handed, often in a dim room. Constraints: no feature, gate or paywall change; iOS conventions; contrast tests; Reduce Motion.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Couch's chrome is the furniture the remote rests on: flat cushions on a ground, separated by gaps, with one warm metal for what you touch next. Refuses the stock iOS grey-and-blue settings look and the glowing "premium dark" remote.
OWN-WORLD: dark is the icon's near-black #0E0E0F with charcoal cushions #1C1C1B and brass #D2AB72; light is linen #ECEAE5 with paper cushions #FBFAF8 and bronze #7E5518. No shadows, gradients, glow or icon tiles; Sora large titles over SF Pro.
STORY: the remote reads first and answers instantly; tabs are four cushions; lists are cushions of rows; brass marks only the selection and the primary action.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Remote tab: TV bar (status dot, name, model and status, latency, switch chevron) at top, the matte charcoal remote full width with the gold d-pad centred, tab cushions at the bottom on the remote's stage, the Remote cushion in brass.
FORM: candidate 3 of 7 (upholstery and soft furnishings). seed:4be48e19f733e1c8.
