Top 10 takeaways

  1. Competitors earn from weekly subscriptions ($3–15/week); users hate it — free controls + cheap one-time Plus is a real opening.
  2. The highest-rated app (4.8) is the one with no purchases and no ads.
  3. Top functional complaint: the remote drops when the phone sleeps or switches apps (fixed this run: T70 silent fast reconnect).
  4. The TV's Allow/PIN prompt blocks people who lost their remote (this run: T71 lost-remote help).
  5. Keyboard and launching apps are the most valued features after the basic buttons.
  6. Casting photos/videos is the most common feature we lack (queued T76, needs a dev build).
  7. Sleep timer and long-press repeat are cheap wins (done this run: T72, free).
  8. Nobody ships a good widget, Watch app, Live Activity or macros — Couch Plus can own that.
  9. Competitors break with Larger Text; Couch's accessibility is a selling point (T74).
  10. Lead store screenshots with the price promise and our own remote, never logos or '100+ brands' claims (T69).

Real app logos: Couch's Apps tab shows each app's real icon as soon as a TV is connected, because the TV itself supplies the image (Roku, LG, Samsung, Sony; see docs/APP_ICONS.md). Demo mode shows original placeholder tiles. Couch never bundles or draws streaming logos (App Review and trademark risk).
