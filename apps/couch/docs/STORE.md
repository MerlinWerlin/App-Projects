**Description:**

Couch is a universal remote for the TVs and streaming players in your home. Every button is free, forever: power, d-pad, touchpad, keyboard, volume, channels, app launcher and as many TVs as you own. No ads, no trials, no weekly plans, no tracking.

- Works over your Wi-Fi with TVs and players running Roku, Samsung Tizen, LG webOS and Google TV / Android TV, with Fire TV in beta.
- Fast: a persistent connection, auto-reconnect when you come back to the app, and Wake-on-LAN for TVs that support it.
- Type with your phone's keyboard, swipe on the touchpad, open apps in one tap.
- Simple mode: big power, volume, channel and home buttons for kids and grandparents.
- Built for accessibility: large targets, haptics, VoiceOver and TalkBack labels.
- Big, clear buttons that grow with your text size, all the way to the largest accessibility sizes.
- Private: no account, no analytics, nothing leaves your home network.

Couch Plus (optional, one-time purchase): Home and Lock Screen widgets, volume in the Dynamic Island, Apple Watch and Wear OS remote, themes and one-tap macros. Nothing on the remote ever needs it.
