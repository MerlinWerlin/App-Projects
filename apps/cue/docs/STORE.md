**Description**
Cue is a teleprompter that keeps up with you instead of racing ahead.

• Voice Pace: the words move only while you're talking and wait when you stop.
• Record with your script right under the camera, so your eyes stay on the lens. Every take goes straight to Photos. No watermark, ever.
• Record one section at a time and retake just the bit you fluffed.
• Fit any script to 30 seconds, a minute, or whatever your video needs.
• Mirror and flip for beam-splitter rigs. Landscape. Big, clear text with a reading line.
• Sections, pauses and private notes that are never read out.
• Import .txt and Word documents. Export caption files timed to your pace.
