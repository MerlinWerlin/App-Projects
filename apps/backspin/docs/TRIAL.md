# Backspin: what to test (Expo Go on your iPhone)

Open Expo Go, then **Backspin** under Recently opened (or scan the QR in `Desktop\Apps\backspin\EXPO_QR.png`). The PC installs and starts it by itself; the first install takes about 10 minutes.

## New since your last test (24 Oct 2026, night)
- Now on Expo SDK 58, so it opens in your Expo Go.
- 10 free goes with everything on, then the lifetime unlock (A$1.99). The home screen shows "N free goes left". In Expo Go the unlock is a pretend test purchase.
  1. Does the free-goes counter count down once per round? (yes/no)
  2. After the 10th go, does Play show the unlock screen instead of a new round? (yes/no)

## First build (24 Oct 2026)
1. Does the 3-card intro and the microphone screen make sense? (yes/no)
2. Play the **daily**. Hold the red button while you say the line. Does it play backwards straight away, through the loud speaker, not the earpiece? (yes/no)
3. Copy the backwards sound, then flip it. Does the score feel fair? Try one good copy and one deliberately bad one. (tell me both numbers)
4. On the result screen, does **Play all four** play original → backwards → your copy → flipped in order? (yes/no)
5. Try the voice effects: chipmunk is free; the others ask for Plus. In Expo Go the Plus unlock is a pretend test unlock. (yes/no)
6. **Share clip**: does WhatsApp or Messages get one audio file with all four parts? (yes/no)
7. **Party**, then **Guess it** with someone: is it fun? Too long, or too short? (comment)
8. Hold to record or tap to record: which do you prefer? (Settings has both.)
9. Any line in the packs that's weird, not funny, or wrong? Tell me the pack and the line.
