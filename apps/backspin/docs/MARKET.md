**What reviewers complain about:** ad spam, paywalls after 1–2 tries, weekly subscriptions they call a scam, broken play/reset buttons, unclear how to play, purchase and restore bugs, watermarks.

**Gaps nobody fills, which Backspin takes:** scoring, party and guess modes, and no subscription all in one app; a daily challenge with streaks; a Wordle-style share line. Still to do: face-cam 4-panel video export (native) and challenge links.

**How the trend is played:** someone sings a famous chorus, the app reverses it, they imitate the gibberish, and it gets flipped. Usually couples, friends or family, filmed face-on, often in split-screen. The laughs come from the mangled result. Backspin never ships copyrighted lyrics; users can freestyle anything themselves.

**Lead lines for the store and marketing:** "No ads. No subscription. Unlimited rounds." · "A real score for your reverse singing" · "Party mode for up to 8".
