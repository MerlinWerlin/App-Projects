Glaze privacy

Last updated 1 November 2026

Data Not Collected

Glaze has no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking. We don't collect, store or share any information about you.

Your photos and videos

Photos and videos you pick to make icons, wallpapers and Live Photos are processed on your iPhone and never leave the device. "Scan my Home Screen" reads app names from your screenshots on the device with Apple's text recognition; the screenshots are never uploaded.

Apps on your iPhone

To pre-select icons for you, Glaze asks iOS only whether 50 specific popular apps are installed (iOS gives no list of your apps). The answer is used only to pre-select icons for those exact apps, which you can untick, and nothing leaves your phone.

When Scan my Home Screen finds an app that isn't in Glaze's catalogue, Glaze may look up that app's name in Apple's public App Store search (itunes.apple.com) to choose a symbol for its kind of app, such as Finance or Food. Only the app name and your store's country code are sent, and only for apps Glaze doesn't already recognise. No screenshots, photos, other app names, identifiers or account details are sent, no sign-in or account is used, and answers are cached on the phone. Without a connection, Glaze picks the symbol on the phone from the app's name, and nothing is sent.

Theme pack downloads

The theme-pack catalogue and art packs are downloaded from GitHub (GitHub Releases) as plain files. No personal data, account or identifier is sent with these downloads; like any website, GitHub sees the network address of the request. Every file is checked against a fingerprint in its pack before use.

App updates

Glaze checks Expo's update service for fixes to the version you have installed. Expo's update library sends the app version and a random installation ID it creates on first launch (not derived from your device, Apple ID or any account; it resets if you delete the app) so it can serve the right update. Glaze adds no personal data to that request.

Purchases

Glaze Plus is bought through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple handles the payment; Glaze only learns whether Plus is unlocked.

Feedback

Sending feedback opens your own Mail app. Nothing is sent unless you press Send there, and only what you write is included.

Questions about privacy: use Send Feedback in the app's Settings.
