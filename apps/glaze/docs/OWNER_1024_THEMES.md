# Owner brief 24 Oct 2026 evening: creative themes (themes-1024)

The owner showed his own home screen: a starry-night wallpaper, white line glyphs on dark rounded
tiles, and large widgets showing planets (blood moon, full moon, Jupiter) whose background is cut
from the wallpaper so the planet floats in the sky. He wants Glaze themes that creative and is
generating theme art separately (AI images) to drop in later. The format below is the contract.

Tasks: T130 format + validator + importer, T131 seamless widget, T132 three starter packs,
T133 seamless widget card on art-pack themes. Nothing here was tested on a device from the cloud.

## 1. Art-pack format (T130)
`themes/<id>/`:
- `wallpaper.png` exactly 1290 x 2796
- `widget-small.png` 510 x 510, `widget-medium.png` 1092 x 510, `widget-large.png` 1092 x 1146:
  transparent hero art (other sizes accepted when the aspect is within 1% and at least half size)
- `palette.json` `{bg, tile, glyph, accent, text}` (hex)
- `meta.json` `{name, mood, tags, credits, licence, generator, prompt}`

Validator `src/core/artPack.ts` (tests in `artPack.test.ts`): PNG sizes, 8-bit non-interlaced,
real transparency on hero art (decodes the pixels), glyph-on-tile and text-on-bg contrast at least
4.5:1, licence allowlist (original, CC0-1.0, public-domain, CC-BY-4.0, openai-output-owned), credits
required (naming the agency) when NASA / ESA / JPL / Webb / Hubble appears or the licence is CC-BY,
no brand or competitor names. Big unquantised files only warn (bundle budget 450 KB wallpaper,
150 KB per hero; hard limits 8 MB / 3 MB).

Import: `node scripts/import-theme-packs.mjs <folder-of-packs> [--dry-run] [--replace]`. Valid packs
are copied into `themes/`, and `src/ui/artPackAssets.ts` + `src/core/artPackLibrary.ts` are
regenerated (a test fails if they drift). Icons stay generated by the icon studio from vector glyphs
in the pack's tile/glyph colours (no per-app AI icons, never brand logos).

Art themes show in a new **Art** row on Themes (free), with the painted wallpaper on cards, the
Home/Lock previews and Save to Photos, and an art credit card on the theme page.

## 2. Seamless widget (T131, T133)
`src/core/seamlessWidget.ts`: device table for current iPhones (widget sizes and home-screen grid
positions in pixels; community-measured, two rows estimated; verify on device), aspect-fill mapping
of the 1290 x 2796 wallpaper to the phone, crop rect in wallpaper pixels, nudge of up to 40 pt.
`SeamlessWidgetPreview` shows it on a mock home screen (works in Expo Go); `SeamlessWidgetControls`
picks size, spot and nudge. On an art theme's page, **Use for my Glaze widget** sends the crop and
the hero art to the native bridge (`setSeamless`), which crops, composites and stores
`seamless-<size>.png` in the App Group; the new **Glaze Seamless** widget (small/medium/large, no
margins) shows it. Expo Go: preview only, with a "needs the TestFlight app" line.
iOS caveats: set the wallpaper with Perspective Zoom off; depth effect / dimming can shift it; the
Lock Screen can't do this; nudge to fine-tune.

## 3. Starter packs (T132)
Drawn by `scripts/brand/make-starter-packs.py` (fixed seeds, no network, original art), imported
with the importer: `starry-night` (milky way, hills; full moon / ringed planet / blood moon),
`sunset-dunes` (setting sun / cloud bank / crescent and evening star), `deep-sea` (jellyfish,
bubbles). 1.2 MB together. The sandbox can't reach NASA image hosts, so no NASA photo yet; the
validator already enforces the credit when one is added.
