Glaze support

Themes, matching icons, widgets and wallpapers for iPhone

Glaze gives your iPhone a look that fits together: pick a theme and you get a wallpaper, icons for your apps and widgets in the same colours. There is no account and no sign-in. Most of the app is free; Glaze Plus is an optional one-time purchase, never a subscription.

Use a theme

  1. Open Glaze and pick a theme on the Themes tab.
  2. Tap Use this theme. Glaze shows three short steps: wallpaper, icons, widgets. You can skip any step and come back from the theme page.

Set the wallpaper

  1. Tap Save wallpaper to Photos.
  2. Open Photos, tap the wallpaper, then Share > Use as Wallpaper, then Add (or Set as Wallpaper Pair).
  3. When iOS asks about the Home Screen, turn Blur off so the icons match.

Install the icons all at once

  1. Choose the apps, then tap Install all icons. Safari opens and says the profile was downloaded. Tap Close.
  2. Open Settings. Near the top, under your name, tap More for Your iPhone or Profile Downloaded (older iOS: General > VPN & Device Management), then View Profile.
  3. Tap Install, enter your passcode, and tap Install again. iOS says "Not Verified" because the profile is made on your iPhone and isn't signed; that is expected.
  4. Go to your Home Screen: the themed icons are there. Move the original icons to the App Library if you like.

The profile only holds Home Screen shortcuts (web clips). It has no device management, VPN, certificates or restrictions, and it never leaves your iPhone. Most icons open their app straight away; a few show a quick "Open in …?" from Safari first.

Install icons one at a time (Shortcuts)

Prefer not to use a profile? On the install screen tap Prefer Shortcuts? One icon at a time. Glaze saves each icon to Photos and shows how to make a shortcut in Apple's Shortcuts app with the "Open App" action and the Glaze image as its icon.

Remove the icons

  • Profile icons: Settings > General > VPN & Device Management > the Glaze icons profile > Remove Profile. All its icons go at once. You can also long-press a single icon and delete it.
  • Shortcuts icons: long-press the icon and delete it from the Home Screen, or delete the shortcut in the Shortcuts app.
  • Your original apps are never changed; drag them back from the App Library.

Add a widget

  1. Long-press the Home Screen > Edit > Add Widget.
  2. Find Glaze, pick a size and tap Add Widget.
  3. Long-press the widget > Edit Widget to choose its art.

Glaze Plus

Glaze Plus is a one-time purchase through the App Store. To get it back on a new iPhone or after reinstalling, open the Plus sheet or Settings and tap Restore purchases. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Live Photo maker

Glaze can turn a short clip of your own into a Live Photo saved to Photos. It moves when you press it in Photos. iPhone often shows Live Photos made by apps as a still wallpaper, so the still wallpapers are the reliable choice.

Privacy

No account, no ads, no tracking, and your photos never leave your iPhone. Read the privacy policy.
