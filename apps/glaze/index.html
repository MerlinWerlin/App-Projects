Top 10 takeaways

  1. Weekly subscriptions dominate; one clear one-time price is our sharpest difference.
  2. Ads, especially one ad per icon or item, are the most hated thing in the category: Glaze has none.
  3. Paywall dark patterns (hidden close button, paywall at launch, paid parts inside 'free' themes) are named in reviews: honest labels win trust.
  4. Icon install friction (one shortcut per app, slow launches, wrong app opening) is the biggest pain: our bulk install + guided flow is the core advantage.
  5. Profile-based icon install may hit App Review guideline 5.5 (one leader dropped it): Glaze now has a one-line switch; recommend off for the first App Store submission.
  6. Theme-matched widget sets are table stakes for 3-in-1 apps and we lacked them: the core generator is now built (T58).
  7. Users browse by mood (cute, neon, anime-style, dark, seasonal) and want big previews: category bubbles queued (T57).
  8. Live wallpapers are fragile on iOS and no app leads them: Live Lock Screen on iOS 26 is an opening if we stay honest about limits.
  9. iOS 26 Liquid Glass looks now sell, and several leaders look dated: v2 design tokens and components are in (T54).
  10. Curated, original, family-safe content stands out against AI filler and NSFW items.
