# Tiler app: Australia and New Zealand research (23 Nov 2026)

The full data, with a source URL on every value, is in `research-au-nz.json`. Values I calculated myself are marked `derived`. Anything I could not confirm is marked `UNVERIFIED`.

## Corrections to the brief (important)
- **AU falls:** NCC 2022 Housing Provisions 10.2.12 sets **1:80 minimum to 1:50 maximum** wherever a floor waste is installed. The old "1:60–1:80 in showers" figure comes from earlier editions and no longer applies. Source: [ABCB](https://ncc.abcb.gov.au/editions/ncc-2022/adopted/housing-provisions/10-health-and-amenity/part-102-wet-area-waterproofing).
- **AS 3958.1-2007 is superseded.** AS 3958:2023, *Installation of ceramic and stone tiles*, replaces both 3958.1 and 3958.2. Source: [HIA](https://hia.com.au/resources-and-advice/building-materials-and-products/use-the-right-building-products-for-the-job/articles/updates-to-ceramic-tile-standard-as-3958-2023).
- **NZ stairs have no 2R+G rule.** D1/AS1 sets a maximum pitch, a maximum riser and a minimum going. BRANZ says not to use the old 2R+G = 580–610 rule of thumb.
- **ARDEX X 56 coverage differs by country.** The ARDEX AU TDS (Mar 2026) gives 12–14 m² (walls, 6 mm notch) and 8–10 m² (floors, 10 mm notch) per 15 kg bag. The ARDEX NZ page gives 8 m² and 6 m².

## Australia
- **Tiles (26 rows):** 600×600 (9–9.5 mm, 3 or 4 per box, 1.08 or 1.44 m²), 300×600 (6/7/8 per box), 400×400, 450×450, 300×300, 200×200, 150×150, 75×300 subway (44 or 50 per box), 100×300, 100×200, 200×300, 200×400, 300×800, 600×1200 (Beaumont, 1.44 m² pack), plus mosaic and hex sheets. Sources are Bunnings product spec blocks and Beaumont product pages. I did not find **800×800 or 1200×1200** stocked.
- **Adhesive:** ARDEX X 56 15 kg (C1 E S2, 12–14 m² walls with a 6 mm notch, 8–10 m² floors with a 10 mm notch); Bostik Excelflex 20 kg (C1S1T, 2.8 kg/m² at a 10 mm notch); Mapei Keraflex Easy S1 20 kg (C2ES1, 4 mm notch 2 kg/m², 6 mm 2.5–3 kg/m², 10 mm 5 kg/m²); Mapei Keraflex Maxi S1 (1.2 kg/m² per mm of bed); Davco Ultraflex 20 kg (C1S1E, 12 m² at a 6 mm notch, plus a notch-by-tile-size guide).
- **Grout:** ARDEX FG 8 (1.5/5/20 kg, kg/m² table). Mapei Ultracolor Plus (5 kg/2 kg, CG2WAF, bulk density 1400 kg/m³). Its formula is **kg/m² = (A+B)/(A×B) × C × D × 1.6**, where A and B are tile length and width, C is tile thickness and D is joint width, all in mm. Mapei Kerapoxy epoxy (2/5/10 kg kits, mix density 1550 kg/m³). Davco Easy Grout premix (2 L/4 L, 1.8 kg/L, coverage table).
- **Membranes:** Davco K10 Plus (4 L/20 L, 20 L ≈ 13.3 m² for 2 coats, ≥0.75 mm wet film per coat, **≥1.0 mm DFT**). ARDEX WPM 155 Rapid Plus (4 L/15 L, 15 L = 17.5 m² at 0.5 mm DFT over 2 coats). Mapelastic AquaDefense (3.5/7.5/15 kg, 1 kg/m² for 2 coats, about 0.4 mm per coat).
- **Primer:** Mapei Primer G (0.08–0.15 kg/m²). ARDEX WPM 300 comes in 4 L/20 L, but AU coverage is unverified.
- **Tape:** ARDEX STB tape is 75 mm × 15 m with a minimum 50 mm end lap; ARDEX STA is a 400 mm × 2.4 m sheet.
- **Puddle flanges:** Kinetic 50/80/100 mm and Wondercap 100×50, 100×80 and 100×100. These come from search results only.
- **NCC 10.2 numbers:**
  - Shower walls waterproof to 1800 mm. Shower wall junctions waterproof 40 mm either side.
  - Unenclosed shower: waterstop at least 1500 mm from the rose.
  - Step-down at least 25 mm. Hob membrane runs at least 50 mm onto the floor. Hobs must not be timber.
  - Perimeter flashing: vertical leg 25 mm above finished floor, horizontal leg 50 mm.
  - Walls beside baths and sinks within 75 mm of the wall: 150 mm above the vessel.
- **Stairs (NCC HP Table 11.2.2a):** riser 115–190, going 240–355, 2R+G 550–700, 2–18 risers per flight. Slip resistance (Table 11.2.4, AS 4586): treads P3 dry / P4 wet; nosing strips P3 / P4.

## New Zealand
- **Tiles (17 rows):** Bunnings NZ carries the same Johnson/Duratile ranges as AU (300×600 at 1.08/1.24/1.26/1.44 m² per box, 600×600 at 1.08/1.44, 300×300, 200×200, 200×300, 150×150, 100×300, 100×200, 75×300, 97×97 sold each, mosaic sheets). The 600×1200 row (Tile Warehouse, 9.5 mm, 2 per box, 1.44 m²) comes from a search result only.
- **Adhesive:** ARDEX X 56 (NZ figures as above) and Mapei Keraflex Maxi S1. **I could not verify a third NZ adhesive.**
- **Grout:** ARDEX FG 8 NZ comes in 5/20 kg. NZ epoxy grout is unverified because the Mapei NZ pages returned 403.
- **Membranes:**
  - ARDEX WPM 001 (6.5/20 kg): floors 7.5 m² at 1.0 mm DFT, walls 15 m² at 0.5 mm DFT, both over 2 coats.
  - ARDEX WPM 155 Rapid (15 L): walls 17.2 m² at 0.5 mm DFT; floors 8.6 m² at 1.0 mm DFT, or 17.2 m² with STB tape.
  - Mapelastic AquaDefense: same as AU.
- **Primer:** ARDEX Multiprime (4/20 L, 6 m²/L) and ARDEX WPM 300.
- **Tape:** ARDEX STB 75 mm × 15 m; Mapeband Easy 130 mm × 10 or 30 m.
- **E3/AS1** (via LEVEL/BRANZ):
  - Tiles must absorb no more than 6% water.
  - Enclosures run up to at least 1800 mm and at least 300 mm above the rose.
  - **Open showers fall at least 1:50** within a 1500 mm radius.
  - E3/AS2 covers membranes (WMA Code of Practice, AS/NZS 4858). NZBC B2 requires 15-year durability.
- **D1/AS1 Table 6** (via BRANZ BU625, 2018):

  | Stair type | Max riser (mm) | Min going (mm) | Max pitch |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Common and main private | 190 | 280 | 37° |
  | Secondary private | 200 | 250 | 41° |
  | Service and minor private | 220 | 220 | 47° |
  | Accessible | 180 | 310 | 32° |

- **NZ slip resistance:** coefficient of friction at least 0.4 on level public access routes (MBIE).

## Unverified or not done
- The AS 3740:2021 clause text is paywalled and I did not read it.
- The current D1/AS1 PDF could not be text-extracted, so the NZ stair values rest on BRANZ (secondary).
- NZ fall for enclosed showers, NZ puddle flanges and NZS 4121 were not confirmed.
- The AU AS 1428.1 nosing strip values (50–75 mm strip, 30% luminance contrast) come from secondary sources only.
- AU and NZ 800×800 and 1200×1200 tiles were not seen. NZ 450×450 was not checked.
- Davco primer coverage and the FG 8 powder density were not found.
