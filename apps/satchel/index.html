Try it on your iPhone

  1. To get Satchel onto TestFlight: create the app in App Store Connect (bundle id com.jared797.satchel, SKU satchel) and send Claude its Apple ID number. Everything else is prepared; see docs/TESTFLIGHT.md.
  2. Install Expo Go from the App Store on your iPhone (Satchel now targets SDK 58, the version Expo Go runs).
  3. On your PC, download the trial kit from this page, unzip it and double-click START_TRIAL.cmd (it installs, runs npx expo install --fix, checks the code, then starts Satchel). If anything is red, paste trial-log.txt into a comment here. The Desktop\Apps\satchel copy is refreshed by every build run that reaches your PC, and your PC's auto-start now installs, checks and serves Satchel on port 8098, so you can open it from Expo Go > Recently opened once it shows ready in Desktop\Apps\_auto\STATUS.txt.
  4. When the QR code appears, scan it with the iPhone Camera app and open it in Expo Go.
  5. Try the demo trip, then turn on flight mode and try again. Comment on this page with anything that looks wrong (docs/TRIAL.md has yes/no questions).

Unzip it on your PC and double-click START_TRIAL.cmd. Once the App Factory overseer can reach your PC it also keeps a copy in Desktop\Apps\satchel.
