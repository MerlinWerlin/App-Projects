Satchel Support

Satchel keeps your trip, tickets, documents and emergency card on your phone, ready for flight mode. Answers to common questions are below.

Questions

Does Satchel work offline or in flight mode?

Yes for everything you have saved: trips, timelines, tickets and QR crops, the hotel address card, the document vault, packing lists, the emergency card, help cards, phrases and low-battery essentials mode are all stored on your phone and need no signal.

For the map, download the city before you travel (Map tab): a downloaded city map, with its toilets and places, works in flight mode. Without one, the map background comes from your phone's built-in map service and needs a signal. The last weather forecast stays on screen offline, marked with when it was fetched.

What does Satchel send over the internet?

Nothing to us: Satchel has no server, account, ads or analytics. When you are online, a few features talk directly to other services: the map background (Apple Maps), weather for your trip's hotel or saved place (MET Norway), toilets or help near a spot that no downloaded city covers (OpenStreetMap), and city map downloads (GitHub). The privacy policy lists exactly what each one receives.

Can I trust the emergency numbers?

Treat them as a starting point and always verify them locally (for example with your hotel, airline, embassy or official government advice) before you rely on them. The list comes with the app so it works offline, but numbers change and some entries have not yet been checked against an official source.

In a real emergency, call the number you know works where you are.

Where is my data stored? How do I back it up?

On your phone only. Satchel has no account and no server, so nothing is stored online by us. Your data is included in your phone's own backup if you use one.

To keep a copy yourself or move to a new phone, open Settings open Settings > Backup and restore, make a backupgt; Backup open Settings > Backup and restore, make a backupgt; Back up and restore, make a backup and save the file somewhere you choose (Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop). Turn on Include documents if you want your vault copies in it. On the new phone, install Satchel and restore from that file. Backup files are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere private.

Can I move a trip to another phone or share it?

Yes. On a trip, tap Export trip and send the .satchel file (AirDrop, Messages, Mail or Files). The other person opens it in Satchel to add the trip. A trip file never contains your vault documents, emergency card or medical notes.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Satchel Plus is a one-time purchase linked to your Apple ID (or Google account). Open Settings > Satchel Plus and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the same account you bought it with. You will not be charged again.

Face ID does not open my document vault

Check that Face ID or a passcode is set up on your phone and that Satchel is allowed to use Face ID (Settings > Satchel on iPhone). If your phone has no Face ID or passcode, the vault cannot be locked and Satchel tells you so. Face ID is checked by your phone; Satchel never receives your face data or passcode.

Satchel does not show my location

Satchel asks for location only when you use a feature that needs it, such as Where am I? on the map or Find toilets near me, and never in the background. If you said no earlier, turn location back on for Satchel in your phone's Settings. GPS can take longer to find you indoors or straight after turning on flight mode. You can also search near your hotel without using your location.

Why are some map points labelled "sample data"?

The demo trip shows invented sample points so you can try the map. They are not real places; do not navigate by them.
