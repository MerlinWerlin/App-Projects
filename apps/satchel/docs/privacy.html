Satchel Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Data Not Collected

In short: Satchel has no accounts, no servers that store your data, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your trips, tickets, documents, packing lists and emergency card are saved on your phone. We never see them.

What stays on your phone

  • Trips, bookings and timelines you add.
  • Tickets, PDFs and images you attach, and any QR code crops you save. Satchel keeps its own copy on your phone; your originals in Files or Photos are not changed.
  • Document vault copies (for example passport, visa or licence copies) and their expiry dates.
  • Your emergency card: insurance details, emergency contacts you pick, and private medical notes.
  • Packing lists, saved places and your settings.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. If you delete Satchel, this data is deleted from your phone with it, unless it is included in your own phone backup.

Location

Satchel checks your location once, only when you tap Where am I? on the map, to show where you are. It never tracks you in the background and does not keep a location history. Your location is not sent to us.

Photos, files and contacts

Satchel only reads a photo, a file or a contact when you pick it. Picked photos and files are copied into Satchel on your phone. For your emergency card, only the contacts you choose are saved, on your phone. Satchel does not upload your photo library, files or address book.

Face ID and passcode

The document vault can be locked with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. iOS (or Android) handles this check. Satchel only learns whether it succeeded; it never receives your face data, fingerprint or passcode.

The map

In this version the map uses your phone's built-in map service (Apple Maps on iPhone), which loads map images over the internet when you have a signal. That service is run by the platform provider under its own privacy policy. Satchel does not send your trips or saved places to it. Points shown on the demo trip are bundled sample data.

App updates

When you are online, Satchel may check for and download an app update from its update service (Expo). This request contains technical details such as the app version and platform, not your trips or any personal information.

Purchases

If you buy the optional one-time upgrade (Satchel Plus), the purchase is handled by Apple (or Google Play on Android). We do not receive your payment details or your name. The app store only tells Satchel that the upgrade is unlocked.

Sync across your devices

Satchel does not sync today. A future version may offer optional sync through your own iCloud account. If you turn it on, your data would be stored in your private iCloud space under Apple's terms, not on any server of ours, and this policy will be updated before that happens.

Sharing

Anything you share from Satchel (for example copying a hotel address into a message) goes only where you send it.

Children

Satchel does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
