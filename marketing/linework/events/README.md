# Linework In-App Events

**Christmas 2026: "Trace your own Christmas cards"** (1–24 Dec 2026)

- `xmas-2026-event-1920x1080.jpg`: event card (16:9).
- `xmas-2026-event-1080x1920.jpg`: details page (9:16).
- `flow-xmas-*-src.jpg`: the originals, 1344 px on the long side. They were made with Nano Banana Pro in Google Flow on 21 Nov 2026, from Linework's real trace screen and its "Starry Christmas tree" template. They show the real camera-tracing method: the phone is held on a desk arm above the card, its screen shows the card and hand through the camera with the outline on top, and the hand draws on the real card underneath. Jared pointed out on 21 Nov that a phone sitting beside the card is not how the app works. The two event images are these originals resized.

Jared said the earlier HTML/PIL renders looked like kids' work, so they were replaced. The images contain no text, because the App Store lays the event name over the bottom.
