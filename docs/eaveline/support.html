Common questions

How do I change my country or units?
Settings › Country. Lengths (metric or feet and inches) and pitch (degrees or rise:12) can also be changed on their own in Settings.
Where do the numbers come from?
Settings › Standards & sources lists the standards, codes and data sheets behind every value for your country, and marks anything that couldn't be verified. Every result also shows how it was worked out.
Are the downpipe numbers a design?
No. They are estimates to plan and order with, using your country's published method. A licensed plumber or designer confirms the real system and overflow.
How does the free trial work?
Tap Start 7-day free trial on the unlock screen. Nothing is charged. After 7 days, the pitch tools and gutter fall stay free; the other calculators and saved jobs need the one-time Full Unlock.
I bought it on another phone
Open the unlock screen and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account.
Where are my jobs kept?
Only on your phone. Use Share job sheet to send a copy anywhere.
