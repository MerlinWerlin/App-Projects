Eaveline Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

Data not collectedEaveline has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Nothing you enter leaves your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • Your country, units and display settings.
  • The roof you're working on and the jobs and results you save.

These are stored only in the app's storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. Sharing a job sheet sends text only where you choose to send it, using the iPhone share sheet.

Motion sensor

The Phone level reads the phone's tilt only while that screen is open, to show a roof pitch. Readings are not stored or sent anywhere.

Purchases

The free trial and the Full Unlock are Apple in-app purchases. Apple handles payment; Eaveline only asks the App Store whether you own them.

Links

Standards and regulator pages open in your browser only when you tap them.

Children

Eaveline is a trade tool and is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here. Questions: see Support.
