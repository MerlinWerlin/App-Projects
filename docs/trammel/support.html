Common questions

How do I start the free trial?

Open any tool with a lock (for example Stairs), or go to Settings > Trammel Full Unlock, and tap Start 7-day free trial. Apple shows the free item; nothing is charged and nothing renews. After 7 days you can unlock every tool with one purchase.

Which tools are free?

Fractions ↔ mm, Square check, Mitre and Roof pitch are free for good, and saved jobs always open.

I bought Full Unlock on another iPhone

Settings > Trammel Full Unlock > Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Can I type feet and inches?

Yes. Any field takes 2400, 2.4m, 240cm, 54 1/2, 54-1/2" or 4' 6 1/2". Switch the units Trammel shows in Settings.

Where do the stair limits come from?

The National Construction Code 2022 (ABCB): Housing Provisions 11.2.2 for houses and Volume One D3D14 for public and private stairways. Trammel's results are guidance: always check them against the plans, the engineer's details and the codes and state variations that apply where you build.
