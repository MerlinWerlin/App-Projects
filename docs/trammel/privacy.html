Trammel Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Data Not Collected

In short: Trammel is a calculator for carpenters. It has no account, no servers, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it makes no network requests of its own. Everything you type and save stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your phone

  • The numbers you type into each calculator, and the last values used, so a tool opens where you left it.
  • Jobs you save and the calculations in them.
  • Your settings: units, fraction size, saw kerf and haptics.

None of this is sent to us or anyone else. Deleting Trammel deletes it, unless it is in your own iPhone backup.

Sharing

If you tap Share job sheet or Share cut list, Trammel hands plain text to the iOS share sheet and you choose where it goes (for example Messages or Mail). Trammel does not see or keep what happens after that.

Purchases

The free trial and Trammel Full Unlock are Apple in-app purchases. Apple handles the payment; Trammel only receives a signed confirmation from the App Store on your phone that the item is owned, to unlock the tools. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Links

This page and the support page open in your browser only when you tap them. GitHub hosts them, and its own privacy policy applies to that visit.

Children

Trammel is a general audience tool and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page is updated and the date at the top changes.
