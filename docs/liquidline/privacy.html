Liquidline Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 November 2026.

Liquidline collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

Your settings (country, units, altitude) and the jobs, notes and readings you save are stored only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Sharing

When you tap Share job sheet, the text goes only where you choose (Messages, Mail and so on), through iOS.

Purchases

The free trial and the Full Unlock are Apple in-app purchases. Apple handles payment; Liquidline only asks the App Store on your iPhone whether you own them. We never see your payment details.

Network

Liquidline works offline. The only network use is the App Store checking purchases and, if you tap the links, opening this page or the support page in your browser.

Children

Liquidline is a professional tool and is not directed at children.
