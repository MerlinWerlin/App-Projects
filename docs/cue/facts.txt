Cue: Teleprompter: facts
========================================

What it is: Cue is a teleprompter for Android phones: write or import a script, keep the words near the camera and record takes without a watermark. Optional Voice Pace scrolls while you talk and waits when you pause.
Category: teleprompter app
Made by: Astro Labs AU (an independent developer in Australia)

Where to get it:
- Google Play (Android): https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.jared797.cue
- Not yet on iPhone or the App Store.

Price: Free download with unlimited scripts and watermark-free recording. Cue Plus is a one-time A$2.99 for Voice Pace, Voice Follow and extras.

Free (no subscription):
- Unlimited scripts
- Steady scrolling with adjustable speed and text size
- Mirror or flip the text for a teleprompter rig
- Countdown before you start
- Record video takes with the script near the camera, with no watermark
- Import scripts with the system file picker

Cue Plus (A$2.99 in Australia; Google Play shows your local price, one-time, not a subscription):
- Voice Pace: the script moves while you talk and waits when you pause
- Voice Follow: on-device speech recognition follows your words (depends on device and language)
- Caption-file export
- Prompter colours
- Word document import
- Rehearsal stats

Trial: Optional free 7-day trial of Plus, started when you choose. It does not charge automatically; scripts and steady-scroll tools stay available when it ends.

Ads: none. Subscription: none. Account: none needed.

Privacy:
- No ads, no account. Scripts and videos stay on your phone.
- Voice Follow uses on-device speech recognition.
- Google Play's Data safety section lists app diagnostics and purchase history. See the listing for the current details.

Not claimed: Voice Follow needs compatible on-device speech recognition and installed language resources; availability varies by device and language. Cue is not yet available on iPhone.
