Scripts

How do I write or import a script?

Tap New script and type or paste. To bring in a file, tap the import button at the top of the Scripts screen and pick a .txt or .md file (free) or a Word .docx file (Cue Plus).

What do the little marks do?
  • # Section starts a section you can jump to and record on its own.
  • *word* makes a word stand out.
  • [pause] or any word in square brackets shows a cue.
  • [[note]] is a private note, never read out.
  • --- adds a break.
How does Fit to time work?

In the editor, tap Fit to time and choose 30 s, 60 s, 90 s, 2 m or 3 m. Cue sets the reading speed so the whole script takes that long. The read time and words per minute show at the bottom.

Prompter and Voice Pace

How do I control the prompter?

Tap to pause or carry on, drag to scroll back, pinch to change the text size. The bar at the bottom has start again, sections, slower and faster, mirror, flip and play/pause.

What is Voice Pace?

Voice Pace listens to the sound level from the microphone. While you talk the words move; when you stop they wait. It never records or uploads anything. You get 3 full Voice Pace reads free; Cue Plus makes it unlimited. Steady scroll is always free.

Can I use a beam-splitter rig?

Yes. Turn on Mirror (left to right) and, if your rig needs it, Flip (upside down). Landscape works too.

Recording

Where do my videos go?

Each take is saved to your Photos the moment you stop. There is never a watermark.

How do I re-record one section?

On the Record screen, tap the section button left of the shutter and choose a section. Cue records just that part and stops at its end.

Voice Pace stopped while recording.

While the camera records sound, Voice Pace pauses and the script uses steady scroll, so the two don't fight over the microphone.

Permissions

I said Don't Allow to the camera, microphone or Photos.

Open the Settings app → scroll down to Cue → turn on Camera, Microphone, or set Photos to Add Photos Only. Cue only uses them on your device. See the Privacy Policy.

Cue Plus

What is Cue Plus?

An optional one-time purchase, not a subscription. It adds unlimited Voice Pace, caption files (.srt and .vtt timed to your pace), prompter colours, Word import and rehearsal stats. The teleprompter, recording and every script stay free.

How do I restore my purchase?

Sign in with the same Apple ID, then open Settings → Restore purchase (or the Cue Plus screen).

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
