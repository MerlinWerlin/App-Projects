Cue compared with typical teleprompter apps

Cue: Teleprompter

Teleprompter apps all scroll a script so you can read it while looking at the camera. They differ in whether recording is watermarked, whether voice scrolling costs a subscription, and how much the free version can do. Here is how Cue compares with what is typical.

When another app may suit you better

  • You need an iPhone app: Cue is Android only for now.
  • You need scripts synced across devices or a team workspace: Cue keeps scripts on the phone with no account.
  • You need a floating prompter over other camera or video-call apps: Cue records inside its own camera screen.
  • You want built-in video editing or AI script writing: Cue focuses on reading and recording.
