Cue Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026 · App: Cue (iOS, com.jared797.cue)

✓
Data Not Collected Cue does not collect, sell or share any data about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your scripts and videos stay on your device.

What Cue does not do

  • No analytics, crash-reporting or advertising tools, and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No sign-up, login or account.
  • No uploads. Your scripts, recordings and voice never leave your device unless you choose to share them.

Camera

Cue uses the camera only on the Record screen, to show the live picture behind your script so you can read and record at the same time. Video is recorded only while you are recording, and each take is saved straight to your Photos library.

Microphone

Cue uses the microphone for two things, both on your device: to record sound with your videos, and for Voice Pace, which listens to how loud the room is so the words move while you talk and wait when you stop. Voice Pace only measures the sound level. It does not recognise words, does not keep a recording (the audio buffer it needs is a temporary file that is thrown away when you stop) and never sends sound anywhere.

Photos

Cue asks for permission only to add your takes to Photos. It cannot see or read the other photos and videos in your library.

Files you import

When you tap Import, you pick a .txt, .md or Word (.docx) file with the system file picker. Cue reads only that file, turns it into a script on your device, and never uploads it.

What is stored, and where

Everything Cue keeps is stored locally on your device, inside the app: your scripts, your settings (text size, colours, countdown and so on), your rehearsal times, how many free Voice Pace reads you have used, and whether you own Cue Plus. None of it is sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Sharing and captions

When you tap Share text or export caption files, Cue hands the text to the system share sheet and it goes only where you choose.

Cue Plus purchase

Cue Plus is an optional one-time purchase. It is handled entirely by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Cue never sees your payment details, name or Apple ID; it only learns whether the purchase went through, so it can switch Plus on and offer Restore purchase.

Feedback

Send feedback opens your own Mail app with our address filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself.

App updates

Store builds of Cue check for small app updates (fixes and wording) when they open, using the Expo EAS Update service. That request contains only technical information needed to deliver the right update, such as the app version, the platform and the update channel, plus a random identifier the update library creates on the device. It contains nothing about you and none of your content or settings. We do not use it to identify or track you.

Children's privacy

Cue is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, so it collects none from children. There are no ads, no chat and no social features. Purchases go through the App Store, where parents can use Ask to Buy.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on a server. Everything Cue keeps is on your device and under your control; deleting the app deletes it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect. We will never start collecting data without saying so here and in the store listing first.
