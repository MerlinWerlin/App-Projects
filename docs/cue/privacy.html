Scripts, video and microphone

Cue stores scripts, preferences and rehearsal information locally. The camera is used on the Record screen; recordings and their sound are saved to Photos. Cue requests permission to add videos and does not browse your photo library. System backups and any Photos sync you enable are controlled by you and Apple.

Voice Pace measures microphone sound levels on your device. Its temporary audio file is deleted when the session stops. Voice Follow uses on-device speech recognition to match spoken words to your script; it refuses recognition when the selected recognizer cannot work on-device. Cue does not upload your voice, transcript, scripts or videos.

Import and sharing

Cue reads only files you select through the system file picker. Sharing text, captions or recordings uses the system share sheet and sends content only to destinations you choose.

Plus and the seven-day trial

Apple processes the optional one-time Cue Plus purchase and the free, user-started 7-day Trial. Cue reads store transaction records to check access and the original trial start date. It does not receive your payment-card details. Ownership and trial dates are cached locally; the trial date also uses the iOS Keychain and may survive reinstallation. Restore uses Apple's original transaction date and does not restart the trial. Nothing is charged automatically when the trial ends.

App updates and diagnostics

Store builds check Expo EAS Update for compatible fixes when they open. Requests contain app and operating-system information, the update channel and a randomly generated installation identifier. The update library can also report failed-update launch information so broken updates can be detected. This is limited technical data for app functionality, disclosed in App Store privacy labels as Device ID and Crash Data. It is not linked to your identity or used for tracking or advertising. Your scripts, recordings and settings are not part of these requests.

Expo processes update-service data on the developer's behalf. See Expo's privacy explanation and privacy policy. Cue has no separate advertising or behavioural analytics service.

Feedback and control

Send feedback opens your mail app; nothing is sent until you send the email. We receive the address and information you choose to include and use them to respond. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence. Deleting Cue removes its app-local scripts and settings; videos already saved to Photos, Apple purchase records, backups and Keychain trial information are managed separately.

Children

Cue has no chat, social network or advertisements. Apple controls purchase approval, including Ask to Buy where enabled. The same limited update-service processing described above applies to all users.

Changes and contact
