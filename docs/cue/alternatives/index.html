Alternatives to subscription teleprompter apps

Cue: Teleprompter

If you record videos to camera and want a teleprompter without a monthly bill or a watermark, there are several routes, from apps with a one-time price to simple tricks.

The main kinds of alternative

  • One-time-purchase teleprompter apps. Apps that keep the core prompter free or charge once instead of monthly. Cue is one of these, on Android.
  • Free apps with a watermark or limits. Common free tiers stamp a watermark on recordings, cap recording length or limit how many scripts you keep.
  • A notes app or document scrolled by hand. Free and simple, but you scroll yourself and your eyes leave the lens.
  • A hardware teleprompter rig. A beam-splitter glass in front of the camera with a phone or tablet underneath. The most natural eye contact; it needs a prompter app that can mirror the text.
  • Bullet points instead of a script. Keep a few key points beside the lens and talk naturally. No app needed, but harder for exact wording.

What to check before you install

  • Is recording watermarked on the free version?
  • Is voice scrolling included, and is it a subscription or a one-time purchase?
  • How many scripts and how long a recording does the free version allow?
  • Does it mirror text for a rig?
  • Does it need an account, and does your script leave the phone?
  • Is it on your platform (iPhone, Android or both)?

Where Cue fits

Cue is an Android teleprompter with free unlimited scripts and watermark-free recording. Cue Plus (A$2.99 once) adds Voice Pace and Voice Follow scrolling, caption export, Word import and more. No ads, no account, no subscription.
