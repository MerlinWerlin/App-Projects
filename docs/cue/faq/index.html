Cue questions and answers

Cue: Teleprompter

Get it on Google Play

Is there a free teleprompter app for Android with no watermark?

Yes. Cue is free on Google Play with unlimited scripts, steady scrolling, mirror and flip, a countdown and video recording with no watermark. No ads and no account.

Is there a teleprompter app that follows your voice?

Cue Plus adds two voice modes: Voice Pace moves the script while you talk and waits when you pause, and Voice Follow uses on-device speech recognition to follow your words. Voice Follow depends on your device and language support.

How much does Cue cost?

The download and core teleprompter are free. Cue Plus is a one-time purchase of A$2.99 in Australia (Google Play shows your local price). There is no subscription.

Is there a free trial of Cue Plus?

Yes, an optional 7-day trial you start from the Plus screen. It doesn't charge automatically, and your scripts and steady-scroll tools stay when it ends.

Can I record video while reading a script?

Yes. Cue records takes with the script near the camera so your eyes stay close to the lens, and recordings have no watermark. Camera and microphone permission are needed.

Can I mirror the text for a teleprompter rig?

Yes. Mirror or flip the text for a beam-splitter teleprompter.

Does Cue work offline?

The prompter, scripts and recording work on the phone with no account. Voice Follow uses on-device speech recognition, which may need the language resources installed first.

Can I import a script?

Yes. Import scripts with the system file picker. Word document import is part of Cue Plus.

Is Cue on iPhone?

Not yet. Cue is Android only for now, on Google Play.

Does Cue have ads or need an account?

No ads and no account. Your scripts and videos stay on your phone.

Can Cue make captions?

With Cue Plus you can export a caption file for your script.
