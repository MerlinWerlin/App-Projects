Plumber's calculator for the job site

Get help

Found a wrong number, a bug, or want a fitting added? Open a support request on GitHub (a free GitHub account is needed). Please include the tool, what you entered and what you expected. Don't include personal details.

Common questions

What's free? Offset, Fall and the Reference cards are free forever. Cut length, Rolling offset, Volume & flow and Cut list are in Full Unlock, a one-time purchase. You can try them all with a free 7-day Trial, started from the Full Unlock screen. Nothing is charged when it ends.

Restore on a new phone: Settings › Full Unlock and restore › Restore purchases.

Inches: tap MM on the Tools screen (or Settings › Units). Inches show to the nearest 1/16″ and you can type 12 3/8 or 4' 2 1/2.

Take-off: the distance from a fitting's centre to where the pipe stops inside it (centre-to-end minus socket depth). Cut length = centre-to-centre measurement minus both take-offs.

Are the fitting sizes exact? They're typical published values. Brands differ, so check the manufacturer's data, or enter your own fitting with the measured sizes.

My data: jobs and results are stored only on your phone. There's no account or cloud copy, so deleting the app deletes them.

Important

Spigot does the maths; you're responsible for the job. Always check against the manufacturer's data, AS/NZS 3500 and your state's plumbing rules.
