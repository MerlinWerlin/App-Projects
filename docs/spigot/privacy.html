Spigot Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026 · App: Spigot (iOS, com.jared797.spigot)

Spigot collects no dataNo account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your jobs and calculations stay on your phone.

What is stored, and where

Spigot keeps your settings (units, appearance), your jobs, the results you save to them, your recent calculations and any custom fittings you enter. All of it is stored inside the app on your iPhone. None of it is sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it, apart from any device backups you keep.

What Spigot does not do

  • No sign-up, login or account.
  • No advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • No access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos.
  • No server of our own. Spigot works fully offline.

Sharing

When you tap Share (a job, a cut list or the working of a calculation) Spigot hands plain text to the iPhone share sheet. It goes only where you choose.

Purchases

The free 7-day Trial and Spigot Full Unlock are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Spigot never sees your payment details, name or Apple Account. It only learns whether you own the items, so it can unlock the tools and offer Restore purchases. The trial's start date is kept on your phone (including in the iPhone Keychain) so the seven days are counted correctly.

Children

Spigot is a trade calculator and collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and its date first, and the App Store privacy details to match.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open a question on GitHub.
