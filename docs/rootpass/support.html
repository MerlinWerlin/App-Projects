Help for the Rootpass iPhone app

My printed template is the wrong size

Print at 100 % or Actual size, never "Fit to page" or "Scale to fit". Measure the 100 mm ruler at the top of every sheet with a tape: it must be exactly 100 mm. If it is not, check your printer's scaling setting and print again.

How do I join a template over several sheets?

Big pipes need more than one sheet. Each sheet is labelled (A1, A2 and so on) and has a dashed trim line. Trim the left edge of each sheet after the first along its trim line, lay it over the previous sheet so the lines run on, and tape it. Check the joined length against the wrap length shown in the app.

How do I use a saddle template?

Square the end of the branch. Cut the paper along the heavy curve, wrap it round the branch with the straight wrap line square to the pipe and the 0° and 360° lines meeting, and scribe along the curve. The numbers on the curve are the cut-back from the square end, so you can check a few with a tape. For a set-on branch choose "Inside of wall" so the bore sits on the header.

Inches or millimetres?

Settings > Units. Inches show decimals and the nearest 1/16". You can type fractions like 3 1/2.

Restore my purchase

Settings > Full Unlock > Restore purchase, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with. Restoring never restarts the free trial.

Are the answers guaranteed?

Every result is worked from published formulas (see Formulas and sources) and shown step by step, and the app is tested against worked examples. It is still a guide for skilled tradespeople: check measurements and fit-up on the job, and follow your design code and engineer's drawings for pressure and structural work.
