Rootpass

Weld & fab calculator for iPhone

Rootpass does the workshop maths for welders, boilermakers and metal fabricators: saddle (fishmouth) templates you print at true scale and tape round the pipe, lobster-back bends, frame mitres, cut lists, steel weight, weld metal, sheet bending and bolt circles. Every answer shows its working.

Saddle templates

Any branch angle, size and offset. Prints true scale over as many sheets as it needs, with a 100 mm check ruler.

Lobster-backs

Cut angle, every piece length and a wrap template for segmented bends.

Cut lists

Fits your parts into the fewest stock lengths, with the blade kerf and end trim counted.

Steel weight

Plate, flat, round, CHS, SHS/RHS and angle in mild steel, stainless and aluminium.

Weld metal

Wire or rods for fillet and butt welds, by process.

Bends and bolt circles

Bend allowance and flat blanks; PCD hole positions with a drilling template.
