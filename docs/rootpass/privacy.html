Rootpass Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026

✓
Rootpass collects no dataNo account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your phone

Measurements you type, saved jobs and settings (units and paper size) are stored only in the app's storage on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them; your phone's own backups are controlled by your iCloud settings.

Templates you print or share

When you print a template or tap "Save or share PDF", Rootpass makes the PDF on your phone and hands it to the iOS print or share sheet. It goes only to the printer, app or person you choose.

Full Unlock and the 7-day trial

Apple processes the optional one-time Full Unlock purchase and the free, user-started 7-day Trial. Rootpass reads Apple's transaction records to check access and the trial's start date; it never receives your payment details. The trial start date is also kept in the iOS Keychain so the trial cannot restart after reinstalling. Nothing is charged when the trial ends.

No updates outside the App Store

Rootpass does not download code. Updates come only through the App Store. It has no server of its own.

Children

Rootpass has no chat, social features or ads. Purchases follow your Apple Account's family settings, including Ask to Buy.
