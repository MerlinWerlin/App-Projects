Couch Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026 · App: Couch (iOS, com.jared797.couch)

✓
Data Not Collected Couch does not collect, sell or share any data about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your TV commands go from your phone straight to your TV on your home Wi-Fi.

What Couch does not do

  • No analytics, crash-reporting or advertising tools, and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No sign-up, login or account.
  • No cloud relay: Couch never sends your button presses, typing or TV list to us or anyone else.
  • Couch does not use your phone's microphone, camera, contacts, photos or location.

Local network

Couch asks for Local Network access so it can find TVs and streaming players on your Wi-Fi and send them the buttons you press. Everything you do on the remote (buttons, touchpad, the text you type into the TV keyboard, launching an app, waking the TV) goes directly from your phone to your TV over your home network.

When you pair, some TVs give Couch a pairing key or certificate so they recognise your phone next time. Couch keeps it on your phone only.

App icons from the internet

To show the real icons of popular streaming apps on the Apps tab, Couch looks up the app's public artwork with Apple's iTunes Search API when your TV doesn't supply an icon. That request contains only the app name being looked up and your country code. It contains nothing about you or your TV. The icons are cached on your phone. Icons your TV supplies come from the TV over your home network.

What is stored, and where

Everything Couch keeps is stored locally on your phone, inside the app: your TVs (the name you gave them, model and network address), pairing keys, favourite and hidden apps, your theme and settings, and cached app icons. None of it is sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes all of it, or remove a single TV from the TVs tab.

Ratings and feedback

Couch may show Apple's own rating prompt after you have used it for a while; Apple handles it. Send feedback or request a feature opens your own Mail app with our address and, so we can help, the app version, your phone model, system version and language filled in. You see everything before you choose to send it.

Purchases

Every remote control in Couch is free. If an optional purchase (Couch Plus or a tip) is offered in future, it will be handled entirely by Apple, and Couch will only learn whether the purchase went through.

App updates

Store builds of Couch check for small app updates (fixes and wording) when they open, using the Expo EAS Update service. That request contains only technical information needed to deliver the right update, such as the app version, the platform and the update channel, plus a random identifier the update library creates on the device. It contains nothing about you and none of your content or settings. We do not use it to identify or track you.

Children's privacy

Couch is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, so it collects none from children. There are no ads, no chat and no social features.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on a server. Everything Couch keeps is on your device and under your control; deleting the app deletes it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect. We will never start collecting data without saying so here and in the store listing first.
