Connecting your TV

How do I add my TV?
  1. Turn the TV on and make sure your phone is on the same Wi-Fi.
  2. Open the TVs tab and tap Add a TV. Couch looks for TVs and streaming players on your network.
  3. Tap your TV. Some TVs ask you to press Allow on the TV screen or type a code shown on the TV.
My TV doesn't show up.
  • Check that the phone and TV are on the same Wi-Fi (not a guest network).
  • On iPhone, make sure Local Network is on: Settings → Couch → Local Network.
  • Some TVs need network control turned on in their own settings first; Couch tells you when it can tell.
  • Still not listed? Tap Enter its address and type the TV's IP address from its network settings.
Can I try it without a TV?

Yes. Turn on Demo mode on the TVs tab to try the remote with demo TVs. Nothing is sent to a real TV.

Using the remote

How do I type on the TV?

Tap the keyboard button on the remote. Letters appear on the TV as you type, and Return sends the search.

Can I control more than one TV?

Yes, as many as you like. Tap the TV name at the top to switch.

It says Reconnecting.

Couch reconnects by itself when you come back to the app. If the TV was off, turn it on; some TVs can be woken over Wi-Fi.
