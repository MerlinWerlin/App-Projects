Alternatives to subscription TV remote apps

Couch: TV Remote

If you've lost the remote, want one remote for several TVs or just want to type searches on your phone, you don't need a weekly subscription. These are the main routes.

The main kinds of alternative

  • Your TV maker's own app. Free and made for that brand, but it only controls that brand, so you need a different app for each TV.
  • One-time-purchase universal remote apps. Pay once and control several brands. Couch is one of these, on iPhone.
  • Free remote apps with ads or weekly plans. Free to install, but many put ads between the buttons or lock basics behind a weekly subscription.
  • A spare or universal physical remote. Works without Wi-Fi or a phone, but has no keyboard and no casting.
  • Your phone's built-in remote. Some phones have a remote in the control centre, but only for their own streaming devices.

What to check before you install

  • Is it a weekly or yearly subscription, or a one-time price?
  • Does the trial start a subscription, and does it charge automatically?
  • Are there ads among the buttons?
  • Does it support your TV (Roku, Samsung, LG, Google TV and so on) over Wi-Fi?
  • Is there a keyboard for searches and passwords?
  • Does it need an account, and what data does it collect?
  • Does it offer Family Sharing?
