Couch compared with typical TV remote apps

Couch: TV Remote

Universal TV remote apps for iPhone all send your phone's taps to the TV over Wi-Fi. They differ in how they charge (weekly plans are common), whether ads sit among the buttons, what the free version can do and what they collect. Here is how Couch compares with what is typical.

When another app may suit you better

  • You use Android: Couch is iPhone only.
  • Your TV isn't on Wi-Fi, or isn't a Roku, Samsung Tizen, LG webOS or Google TV / Android TV set: Couch doesn't use infrared, and Fire TV support is still in beta.
  • You only have one brand of TV and want it free: your TV maker's own app may be free for that brand.
  • Your physical remote is lost and your TV asks you to press Allow on it the first time: some TVs need that once before any phone app can connect.
