Alternatives to subscription AR drawing apps

Linework: Trace & Sketch

If you want to trace a photo or drawing onto paper but don't want a weekly subscription, you have several options, from apps with a one-time price to methods artists have used for centuries.

The main kinds of alternative

  • One-time-purchase tracing apps. Apps that show the picture over the camera view and charge once (or keep core tracing free) instead of weekly. Linework is one of these.
  • Free apps with ads. These cost nothing up front but often show ads, sometimes mid-drawing, and may limit tracing your own photos.
  • Lightbox or tracing pad. A light pad under thin paper. Very steady and no phone needed; it costs a little and only works with paper thin enough to see through. A tablet or phone screen at full brightness does the same job for small drawings.
  • A sunny window. Tape the picture and your paper to a window. Free and steady, but vertical and daylight only.
  • The grid method. Draw a grid over the picture and a matching grid on your paper, then copy one square at a time. Slower, but it works at any size and trains your eye.
  • Carbon or graphite transfer paper. Print the picture, lay transfer paper under it and go over the lines. Good for wood, canvas and walls.
  • A projector. Projects the picture onto a wall or large surface; useful for murals, but costs more and needs a dark room.

What to check before you install

  • Is the price weekly, yearly or one-time? Check the in-app purchase list on the store page before starting a trial.
  • Can you trace your own photo for free, or only the app's templates?
  • Does the picture stay still? Look for a lock, a touch lock and reviews that mention drift.
  • Are there ads, and does the app need an account?
  • Does it work without the camera (a lightbox mode) for thin paper?
  • What does the privacy label say?

Where Linework fits

Linework is a one-time-purchase AR tracing app for iPhone and Android. Tracing your own photos with every tracing tool stays free with no ads; Linework Plus (A$2.99 once) adds the full library of 1,900+ templates and photo to outline. It also has a lightbox mode for tracing through thin paper on the screen.
