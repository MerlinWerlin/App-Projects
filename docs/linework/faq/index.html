Linework questions and answers

Linework: Trace & Sketch

Get it on App StoreGet it on Google Play

Is there an AR drawing app without a subscription?

Yes. Linework has no subscription. Tracing your own photos with every tracing tool and 24 starter templates stays free, with no ads. Linework Plus is a one-time A$2.99 (local price shown in the store) for the full template library and photo to outline. There is an optional free 14-day trial that doesn't charge when it ends.

How much does Linework cost?

The download is free. Linework Plus is a one-time in-app purchase of A$2.99 in Australia; the App Store and Google Play show the price in your currency. There is no weekly, monthly or yearly plan.

How do I trace a picture from my phone onto paper?

Open the picture in Linework, stand the phone securely above your paper on a glass, a stack of books or a phone stand, then draw along the lines you see on the screen. Fade the picture with the opacity slider until you can see both it and your pencil.

How do I stop the picture moving while I trace?

Tap Lock so a stray touch can't move it, and turn on touch lock so your hand can rest on the screen. On iPhone, focus also stays put while locked. Keep the phone on a stable stand; most drift comes from the phone itself moving.

Does Linework project the image onto the paper?

No. Like other AR tracing apps, it shows the picture on your phone screen over the camera view. You look at the screen and draw on the paper underneath.

Can I trace without using the camera?

Yes. Lightbox mode shows the picture full-bright on a white screen so you can lay thin paper over the phone or tablet and trace it. No camera access is needed.

Can I turn a photo into a sketch to trace?

Yes, with Linework Plus or during the trial. Photo to outline makes line art on the phone in several styles with detail and line-thickness controls. Nothing is uploaded.

Is tracing cheating?

No. Artists have traced with projectors, lightboxes and grids for centuries. Tracing helps you learn proportions and line confidence; Linework's Sketch steps reveal a picture from big shapes to fine lines so you build it the way artists do.

Is Linework on iPad?

Not at the moment. On Apple devices Linework runs on iPhone (iOS 16.4 or later) and on Macs with Apple silicon. On Android it is on Google Play.

Does Linework have ads or need an account?

No ads and no account.

What does Linework do with my photos?

Your photos, outlines and drawings stay on your device, and image processing happens on the device. The App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected; Google Play's Data safety section lists app diagnostics, device IDs and purchase history.

Can I record a timelapse of my drawing?

Yes. Linework records a timelapse of your drawing and saves it to Photos when you choose. On iPhone it records up to 10 minutes with no watermark, and the guide picture isn't recorded.

What can I trace with it?

Your own photos, plus 1,900+ original templates with Plus: kawaii and chibi, tattoo flash, animals, sea life, fairy tales, flowers, mandalas, letters in 16 styles, and a Workshop of exact angles, joints and layouts for woodwork and trades.

What languages is Linework in?

English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese, Hindi and Arabic.
