Linework: Trace & Sketch: facts
========================================

What it is: Linework shows a photo or template over your phone's live camera view so you can trace it onto real paper with your own pencil, or use the screen as a lightbox.
Category: AR drawing and tracing app
Made by: Astro Labs AU (an independent developer in Australia)

Where to get it:
- App Store (iOS): https://apps.apple.com/au/app/id6826041969
- Google Play (Android): https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.jared797.linework
- On Apple devices it runs on iPhone (iOS 16.4 or later) and on Macs with Apple silicon; it is not offered for iPad.

Price: Free download. Tracing your own photos with every tracing tool stays free; Linework Plus is a one-time A$2.99 for the full template library and photo-to-outline.

Free (no subscription):
- Trace your own photos with the camera overlay
- Opacity, move, resize and rotate
- Picture lock and touch lock so the image stays put while your hand rests on the screen
- Grid, mirror and flashlight
- Lightbox mode: trace through thin paper laid on the screen, no camera needed
- 24 starter templates

Linework Plus (A$2.99 in Australia; Google Play and the App Store show your local price, one-time, not a subscription):
- The full library of 1,900+ original templates, including letters in 16 styles and Workshop angles, joints and layouts
- Photo to outline: turn a photo into line art on the phone

Trial: Free 14-day trial of Plus. It does not charge when it ends; tracing your own photos stays free afterwards.

Ads: none. Subscription: none. Account: none needed.

Privacy:
- App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected.
- No ads, no account, no tracking SDKs. Photos, outlines and drawings stay on your device; image processing happens on the device.
- Google Play's Data safety section lists app diagnostics, device IDs and purchase history (used for analytics, app functionality and purchases). See the listing for the current details.

Not claimed: Linework shows the picture on your phone screen over the camera view; it does not project an image onto the paper. It needs a stable stand (a glass, a stack of books or a phone stand) for camera tracing.
