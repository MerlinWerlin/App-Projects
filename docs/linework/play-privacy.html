Linework Privacy Policy

Google Play Android version · Package: com.jared797.linework
Effective date: 4 November 2026

Linework processes your pictures and drawings on your device. We do not run an image-upload service, an app-account system, an advertising service or a developer analytics backend.

The Android app uses Google Play for optional purchases. Google’s billing library also sends technical diagnostics to Google, including when a store connection or API call fails. This can happen without a purchase.

Pictures, camera and recordings

You choose a picture through Android’s system picker. Linework imports the selected picture and makes outlines on your device. Its camera shows the live view behind your tracing overlay. Recording starts when you choose Record, and a clip is written to your Photos library when you choose Save to Photos. Linework does not use the microphone.

Linework does not send your photos, camera frames, outlines, drawings or recording files to us or to an image-processing server. Lightbox works without camera access.

Data kept on your device

The app stores settings, recent images, favourite templates, saved drawings, drawing statistics, the local trial start date and a cached Plus entitlement. You can remove recent images and drawings in the app. Uninstalling removes local app data; exported recordings and system-managed backups may remain.

Android may back up or transfer app data according to your own backup and device-migration settings. We do not receive those backups. Manage exported files and backups through your device, Photos and backup settings.

Google Play purchases

The 14-day Android evaluation starts only when you choose it and stores its start date locally. It does not automatically charge you. Plus is an optional one-time Google Play purchase.

Google processes payment and purchase records under its own terms. Linework receives the product, purchase status and transaction information needed to unlock, acknowledge or restore Plus. We do not receive your payment-card details, and we do not send purchase records to a server we operate.

Technical billing diagnostics sent to Google

Google’s billing library records API usage, success and failure information and connection problems to improve billing performance and error support. Its diagnostic metadata includes the app package and version, billing-library and Android versions, device brand, manufacturer, model, operating-system build, memory information and a random billing-client session identifier. Transport-related technical information can accompany the request.

These requests use Google’s encrypted HTTPS transport. They do not contain picture or drawing files supplied by Linework. The random session identifier is not an advertising ID or an identifier that Linework maintains across installations.

We do not operate the server that holds Google’s diagnostics, set its retention period or directly delete its records. Google handles that information under its Privacy Policy and applicable service terms. Google’s billing-library documentation describes its technical logging.

Other services and updates

This Google Play version uses the code packaged with the app; Expo over-the-air updates are disabled. Android app updates are delivered through Google Play. The app has no ads, chat or app sign-in.

Children and family use

Linework is a general-audience drawing aid. It does not ask children for a name, email address or pictures to upload. Google Play’s purchase controls can be used by parents. The technical billing processing described above also applies when the app is used by a family.

Questions and changes

Linework is provided by Jared James Bradley, publishing as Astro Labs AU. Contact jaredbradley272@gmail.com with privacy questions. We can help with app-held local data and explain our handling; Google manages its own payment and diagnostic records.

Changes will be described on this page with an updated effective date.
