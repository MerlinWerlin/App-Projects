Effective 23 November 2026

What Groutline stores

Your country, unit and haptics settings and any jobs you save are stored only on your device. Groutline has no server of its own and sends nothing anywhere. It uses no camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

Sharing

If you tap Share on an answer, Groutline opens the system share sheet with a plain-text copy of the answer and its working. It goes only where you choose to send it.

Full Unlock and the 7-day trial

Apple processes the optional one-time Full Unlock purchase and the free, user-started 7-day Trial. Groutline reads your App Store transaction records to check access and the original trial start date. It never receives your payment details. Access and the trial start date are cached on your device; the trial date is also kept in the iOS Keychain and may survive reinstalling. Restore uses Apple's original transaction date and does not restart the trial. Nothing is charged automatically when the trial ends.

Updates and diagnostics

Groutline does not download code outside the App Store. It has no analytics, crash reporting or advertising SDKs.

Deleting your data

Delete a saved job from the Jobs tab, or delete the app to remove everything it stored on the device. Apple purchase records and the Keychain trial date are managed by iOS.

Children

Groutline has no chat, social features or ads. Purchases follow your Apple account settings, including Ask to Buy.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy and its date if our practices change. Questions: see Help & support.
