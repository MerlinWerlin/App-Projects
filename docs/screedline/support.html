Common questions

How accurate are the quantities?

The maths is exact for the shapes and numbers you enter, and every answer shows its working. Real jobs vary: uneven sub-base, form bulge, spillage, compaction and material moisture. Set a sensible waste allowance and confirm orders with your supplier, and anything structural with your engineer.

Where do the formulas and densities come from?

Settings > Sources and formulas lists them: Australian Standards and the NCC (via published summaries), CCAA guidance, AUS-SPEC, manufacturers' data sheets and Engineering ToolBox. Densities, laps, bag yields and truck sizes can all be changed to match your supplier.

Can I use feet and inches?

Yes. Settings > Units > Imperial. Inputs and answers switch to feet, inches and cubic yards. The working is shown in metric because the conversions are exact.

How does the free trial work?

On the Full Unlock screen, tap Start free 7-day trial and confirm the free item with Apple. Every tool opens for seven days. It never turns into a charge. Full Unlock is a single payment, not a subscription.

I bought Full Unlock on another iPhone

Settings > Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

Are my jobs backed up?

Jobs are saved on your iPhone and included in your normal iPhone or iCloud backup. Screedline has no account or server, so share a job sheet if you want a copy elsewhere.
