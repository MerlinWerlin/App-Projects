Screedline Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026

Screedline collects no data.No account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your jobs and calculations stay on your iPhone.

What stays on your phone

Calculations, saved jobs, notes, checklist ticks and settings are stored only in the app's storage on your device. Screedline has no server of its own and does not upload anything. Deleting the app deletes them, apart from what your own iPhone or iCloud backup keeps.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the system share sheet opens and the job sheet goes only where you send it, for example Messages or Mail.

Purchases and the free trial

Apple processes the optional one-time Full Unlock purchase and the free, user-started 7-day Trial. Screedline reads your App Store transaction records on the device to check access and the trial's original start date. It never receives your payment details. The trial date is also kept in the iOS Keychain so reinstalling does not restart it. Nothing is charged when the trial ends.

Permissions

Screedline does not ask for location, camera, photos, contacts, microphone or notifications.

Children

Screedline has no chat, social features or advertising. Purchases follow your Apple account and family settings, including Ask to Buy.
