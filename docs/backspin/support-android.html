Backspin Android Support

The Android release is in preparation. This guide describes the planned Google Play version.

Record, reverse and copy

Say a short line, listen to it backwards, then copy that sound. Backspin flips your attempt forwards and compares its sound, rhythm and length.

Allow microphone access when Android asks. In hold mode, hold Record for your whole line and release to finish. You can change recording controls in Settings. Recording and scoring happen on your phone.

Microphone or playback trouble

Open Android Settings, find Apps → Backspin → Permissions, and allow the microphone. Menu names vary by phone. Return to Backspin and start a fresh take.

If a take is too quiet or short, move closer to the microphone and record the whole line. For playback, check media volume and the selected speaker, headphones or Bluetooth device. Backspin records during a round after you start recording.

Free goes and Plus

Your first 10 scored rounds include all packs, effects and party sizes. The Play tab shows your remaining goes. A party started within the free allowance can finish. Saved takes remain playable and shareable when the allowance runs out.

Backspin Plus is one optional lifetime purchase for unlimited rounds. There is no subscription or advertising. Google Play shows your local price before payment.

To restore Plus, use the Google Play account that bought it, then tap Restore purchase on the Plus screen or Restore purchases in Settings. Purchases and restore need Google Play and an internet connection.

If payment is pending, complete it in Google Play, then restore. If the app cannot confirm a purchase, check Google Play order history before trying again. For refunds, see Google Play's refund options or contact us below.

Saved takes and sharing

Save take keeps the original and your copy in private app storage. Open Takes to play, share or delete a saved take. Share clip opens Android's share sheet so you choose the receiving app.

Clearing app data or uninstalling removes local data, subject to Android backups and copies you have shared or exported. Backspin has no user accounts. See the Android privacy policy for details.
