Backspin Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026 · App: Backspin (iOS, com.jared797.backspin)

✓
Data Not Collected Backspin does not collect, sell or share any data about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your recordings stay on your phone unless you share them.

What Backspin does not do

  • No analytics, crash-reporting or advertising tools, and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No sign-up, login or account.
  • No uploads. Your voice recordings never leave your phone unless you tap Share clip and choose where to send them.

Microphone

Backspin records your voice only while you hold the record button (or between your two taps, if you switch to tap to record). The recording is reversed, compared and scored on your phone. Nothing is listened to in the background, nothing is recognised as words, and nothing is uploaded.

The sounds from each round are kept in a temporary folder inside the app and cleared when the round ends. A round is only kept if you tap Save take; saved takes live in the app's own storage on your phone and you can delete them on the Takes tab.

Sharing

When you tap Share clip, Backspin builds the four-part clip on your phone and hands it to the system share sheet. It goes only where you choose. Share result shares a line of text with your score.

What is stored, and where

Everything Backspin keeps is stored locally on your phone, inside the app: your settings, your rounds played, best score and daily streak (this is also how the 10 free goes are counted), saved takes, the party player names you typed, and whether you own Backspin Plus. None of it is sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Backspin Plus purchase

Backspin Plus is an optional one-time purchase. It is handled entirely by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Backspin never sees your payment details, name or Apple ID; it only learns whether the purchase went through, so it can unlock the app and offer Restore purchase.

Feedback

Send feedback or ideas opens your own Mail app with our address, the app version, your phone's system version and your rounds played filled in, so you can see everything before you choose to send it.

App updates

Store builds of Backspin check for small app updates (fixes and wording) when they open, using the Expo EAS Update service. That request contains only technical information needed to deliver the right update, such as the app version, the platform and the update channel, plus a random identifier the update library creates on the device. It contains nothing about you and none of your content or settings. We do not use it to identify or track you.

Children's privacy

Backspin is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, so it collects none from children. There are no ads, no chat, no accounts and no social features; party names stay on the phone. Purchases go through the App Store, where parents can use Ask to Buy.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on a server. Everything Backspin keeps is on your device and under your control; deleting the app deletes it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect. We will never start collecting data without saying so here and in the store listing first.
