Backspin Android Privacy Policy

Prepared 4 November 2026 for the first Google Play release. App: Backspin, com.jared797.backspin. Developer: Jared James Bradley.

Recordings and microphone

Backspin records during a round after you grant microphone permission and start recording. It does not listen in the background or transcribe your words. Recording, reversing, comparing, scoring and building share clips happen on your phone. Recordings are not uploaded to the developer.

Round audio uses temporary app storage. Save take keeps the original and copy in private app storage. Scores, preferences and party names are stored locally. Delete saved takes in the Takes tab. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes local data, subject to Android device backups and copies you have exported.

Sharing and feedback

Share clip opens Android's share sheet. You choose the receiving app, whose privacy practices then apply. Backspin does not choose a recipient or automatically send your clip. Feedback opens your email app with an editable message containing the app version, system version and round count. Nothing is sent unless you send it. If you email us, we receive the message and sender details and use them to answer your request.

Purchases

Backspin Plus is an optional one-time lifetime purchase through Google Play. Google processes payment under its privacy policy. Backspin uses purchase status and transaction information supplied by Play Billing to unlock Plus and restore a purchase. The developer does not receive your card details through the app. Google Play account controls govern purchases and parental approval.

Technical billing diagnostics

Google's billing library also sends technical diagnostics to Google. Its metadata includes the app package and version, billing-library and Android versions, device brand, manufacturer, model and system build, memory information and a random billing-client session identifier. API success, failure and connection information helps Google improve billing performance and support errors. Transport-related technical information can accompany these requests.

These requests use Google's encrypted HTTPS transport. Backspin does not pass recordings or party names to the billing library. We do not operate Google's diagnostic servers, set their retention period or directly delete their records. Google handles that information under its Privacy Policy and applicable service terms. Google's billing-library documentation describes its technical logging.

Updates and technical services

This Android release contains its app code in the installed bundle, with Expo over-the-air updates disabled. It does not use the iOS version's EAS Update requests, random EAS installation identifier or update-service crash reporting. There are no advertising SDKs, Backspin user accounts or developer-hosted recording servers. In addition to the billing diagnostics above, Google Play and Android may separately process installation, purchase and diagnostic information according to your Google account and device settings.

Your choices

You can revoke microphone permission in Android Settings, delete saved takes, clear app storage and choose whether to share or send feedback. Backspin does not ask children to send personal information or recordings to the developer, and has no chat or advertising features. Account deletion is unnecessary because Backspin has no user accounts.

Contact and changes

Contact jaredbradley272@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests concerning email you have sent us. This page and the store disclosure will be revised if Android data practices change.
