Alternatives to ad-heavy reverse audio apps

Backspin: Reverse Your Voice

The reverse singing challenge is simple: record a line, play it backwards, copy the backwards sound, then flip your copy to hear how close you got. You can play it with several kinds of tool.

The main kinds of alternative

  • One-time-purchase reverse singing games. Apps built for the challenge, with rounds and scoring, that charge once instead of weekly. Backspin is one of these, on Android.
  • Free reverse audio apps with ads. Record and reverse at no cost, but ads often interrupt and features may sit behind a weekly plan.
  • A free audio editor on a computer. Desktop audio editors can reverse any recording. Free and precise, but slow for a party.
  • Social video app effects. Some video apps offer a reverse effect for clips. Handy for posting, with no scoring.

What to check before you install

  • Weekly subscription or one-time price? Check the in-app purchase list.
  • How many ads, and do they play before each recording?
  • Does it score your attempt, or just play it back?
  • Can a group play on one phone?
  • Does it upload your recordings?

Where Backspin fits

Backspin is a reverse-voice party game for Android with scoring, a daily challenge and party modes for up to 8 players. The first 10 scored rounds are free with everything on; Backspin Plus is a one-time A$1.99 for unlimited rounds. No ads, no subscription, recordings stay on the phone.
