Backspin: Reverse Your Voice: facts
========================================

What it is: Backspin is a reverse-voice party game for Android: say a short line, hear it backwards, copy the backwards sound, and Backspin flips your copy forwards and scores how close it is.
Category: reverse singing and reverse audio game
Made by: Astro Labs AU (an independent developer in Australia)

Where to get it:
- Google Play (Android): https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.jared797.backspin
- Not yet on iPhone or the App Store.

Price: Free download with 10 scored rounds and everything switched on. Backspin Plus is a one-time A$1.99 for unlimited rounds. No ads, no subscription.

Free (no subscription):
- 10 scored rounds with every pack, effect and party size switched on
- Quick round, daily challenge, freestyle and party mode
- Loop or slow down the backwards clip while you practise
- Saved takes stay playable and shareable

Backspin Plus (A$1.99 in Australia; Google Play shows your local price, one-time, not a subscription):
- Unlimited scored rounds
- Every challenge pack (950+ lines)
- All voice effects
- Party mode for up to 8 players
- Unlimited saved takes and 30-second takes

Trial: No trial needed: the first 10 scored rounds are free with everything on.

Ads: none. Subscription: none. Account: none needed.

Privacy:
- No ads, no account. Recording and audio processing happen on your phone; Backspin doesn't upload your recordings, and you choose when to share them.
- Google Play's Data safety section lists app diagnostics and purchase history. See the listing for the current details.

Not claimed: Backspin ships no copyrighted song lyrics; you sing or say your own lines or use its built-in challenge lines. It is not yet on iPhone.
