  • Cue: Teleprompter
    Cue is a teleprompter for Android phones: write or import a script, keep the words near the camera and record takes without a watermark. Optional Voice Pace scrolls while you talk and waits when you pause.
    Free download with unlimited scripts and watermark-free recording. Cue Plus is a one-time A$2.99 for Voice Pace, Voice Follow and extras.

  • Backspin: Reverse Your Voice
    Backspin is a reverse-voice party game for Android: say a short line, hear it backwards, copy the backwards sound, and Backspin flips your copy forwards and scores how close it is.
    Free download with 10 scored rounds and everything switched on. Backspin Plus is a one-time A$1.99 for unlimited rounds. No ads, no subscription.
