Glaze compared with typical Home Screen theme apps

Glaze: Icon Themes & Widgets

Home Screen theme apps for iPhone sell wallpapers, icon packs and widgets. They differ in how they charge (weekly plans are common), whether ads sit in the app and whether one theme covers everything. Here is how Glaze compares with what is typical.

When another app may suit you better

  • You use Android: Glaze is iPhone only.
  • You want a widget builder with data and layouts you design yourself: Glaze widgets show theme artwork and your own pictures.
  • You'd rather not have any subscription: Glaze Plus is yearly, though the 10 free themes need none.
