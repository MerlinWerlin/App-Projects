Glaze privacy

Last updated 15 November 2026 (Glaze Plus subscription; no over-the-air updates from 1.0.42)

Data Not Collected

Glaze has no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking. We don't collect, store or share any information about you.

Your photos and videos

Photos and videos you pick to make icons, wallpapers and Live Photos are processed on your iPhone and never leave the device. "Scan my Home Screen" reads app names from your screenshots on the device with Apple's text recognition; the screenshots are never uploaded.

Apps on your iPhone

To pre-select icons for you, Glaze asks iOS only whether 50 specific popular apps are installed (iOS gives no list of your apps). The answer is used only to pre-select icons for those exact apps, which you can untick, and nothing leaves your phone.

When Scan my Home Screen finds an app that isn't in Glaze's catalogue, Glaze may look up that app's name in Apple's public App Store search (itunes.apple.com) to choose a symbol for its kind of app, such as Finance or Food. Only the app name and your store's country code are sent, and only for apps Glaze doesn't already recognise. No screenshots, photos, other app names, identifiers or account details are sent, no sign-in or account is used, and answers are cached on the phone. Without a connection, Glaze picks the symbol on the phone from the app's name, and nothing is sent.

Calendar and weather widgets

Glaze asks for these only if you add the Next event or Weather widget and tap it to set it up; you can say no and every other part of Glaze works the same.

Next event reads your calendars with Apple's EventKit, on your iPhone, to show your next event's title, time and calendar colour in the widget. Your events never leave the device and Glaze does not store or send them.

Weather uses your approximate location (asked as "While Using the App"). The coordinates are rounded to two decimal places (about 1 km) and sent only to Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com), a free weather service, to get the current temperature and today's forecast; no account, key, name or identifier is sent with it. Glaze's developer never receives your location, and only the rounded coordinates and the last forecast are kept, on your iPhone, for the widget. Open-Meteo, like any website, sees the network address of the request; see open-meteo.com for its own terms. Weather data by Open-Meteo.com, licensed CC BY 4.0.

Theme pack downloads

Theme images and the theme catalogue are plain files (images and JSON, no code) fetched from our storage on Cloudflare (Cloudflare R2, delivered by Cloudflare's CDN). No personal data, account or identifier is sent with these downloads, and nothing about you is stored; like any website, Cloudflare sees the network address of the request. Older versions of Glaze fetch the same kind of files from GitHub. Every file is checked against a fingerprint in its pack before use. A few complete themes are built into the app and need no download at all.

A few older Space themes use a real telescope photo. If you open one, Glaze downloads that one photo from the official ESA/Webb image site (cdn.esawebb.org) as a plain image download, with nothing about you added.

App updates

From version 1.0.42, Glaze does not download app code or updates outside the App Store. Updates come only through the App Store (or TestFlight while testing), and Glaze sends nothing to any update service. Earlier test versions checked Expo's update service for fixes; those requests carried only the app version and a random installation ID, never your data.

Purchases

Glaze Plus is bought through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple handles the payment; Glaze only learns whether Plus is unlocked.

Installing icons

"Install all at once" builds a configuration profile on your iPhone that holds only Home Screen web clips, one per app you chose. Glaze hands it to Safari from your own iPhone (address 127.0.0.1), so it never goes over the internet. You install it yourself in Settings and can remove it there at any time (Settings > General > VPN & Device Management). The Shortcuts method uses Apple's Shortcuts app and sends nothing.

Glaze Plus subscription

Glaze Plus is a yearly subscription bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment and your account; Glaze only asks Apple, on your iPhone, whether the subscription is active. Glaze receives no payment details and collects no data for it.

Everything Glaze connects to

  • Cloudflare (R2 storage and CDN): theme images and the theme catalogue (files only, no identifiers). Older app versions use GitHub for the same files.
  • Apple's public App Store search (itunes.apple.com): only the name of an app Glaze doesn't recognise, plus your store's country code.
  • ESA/Webb image site: only if you open an older Space theme.
  • Apple's App Store (StoreKit): only when you buy or restore Glaze Plus.
  • Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com): only if you use the Weather widget, with your location rounded to about 1 km.

None of these requests carry your name, email, Apple ID, contacts, calendar, photos or usage history, and none are used for tracking or advertising. The only location ever sent is the rounded one in the Weather widget's own request to Open-Meteo, which Glaze's developer never receives. That is why the App Store page says "Data Not Collected".

Children

Glaze is rated 4+, has no accounts, chat, user-generated content or ads, and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Feedback

Sending feedback opens your own Mail app. Nothing is sent unless you press Send there, and only what you write is included.

Questions about privacy: use Send Feedback in the app's Settings.
