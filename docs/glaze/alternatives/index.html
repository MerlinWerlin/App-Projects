Alternatives to weekly-subscription theme apps

Glaze: Icon Themes & Widgets

If you want a Home Screen that matches, you don't need a weekly plan. These are the main routes.

The main kinds of alternative

  • Do it yourself. Free: save a wallpaper and make each icon in Apple's Shortcuts app. It takes a while for a full screen.
  • Icon-pack apps. Sell sets of icons; you still find widgets and a wallpaper to match.
  • Widget apps. Build or style widgets; icons and wallpaper are up to you.
  • All-in-one theme apps. One theme covers wallpaper, icons and widgets. Glaze is one of these, on iPhone.
  • Apple's own options. Dark and tinted icons and Focus Home Screens change the look a little, without new art.

What to check before you install

  • Is it weekly, yearly or one-time, and what does the free version keep?
  • Does a trial roll into a weekly plan?
  • Are there ads, or ads to unlock icons?
  • Does one theme include the wallpaper, icons and widgets?
  • Does it show how to undo the icons?
  • Does it need an account, and what data does it collect?

Where Glaze fits

Glaze is an all-in-one Home Screen theme app for iPhone: over 250 original themes, each with a wallpaper, matching icons and five widget artworks. 10 premium themes are free to keep; Glaze Plus is A$2.99 a year for every theme. No ads, no account, no tracking.
