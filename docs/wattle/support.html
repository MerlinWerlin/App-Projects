Common questions

Is Wattle a replacement for AS/NZS 3000 or 3008?

No. Wattle is a calculation aid that gives estimates. Always check against AS/NZS 3000, AS/NZS 3008, the manufacturer’s data and the job’s design. Electrical work in Australia and New Zealand must be done by a licensed electrician.

Where do the formulas come from?

Open Tools › Safety & sources in the app. Every result also has Show the working, so you can check each step.

Why doesn’t Wattle include AS/NZS 3008 tables?

Those tables are copyright. Use the mV/A·m method with the value from AS/NZS 3008 or your cable maker, or the From size estimate, which works from conductor resistivity and temperature.

How do the trial and Full Unlock work?

Offsets, Ohm’s law, power and the converter are free. Tools › Full Unlock lets you start a free 7-day trial (Apple asks you to confirm a free item; nothing is ever charged automatically). After that, one payment unlocks every calculator for good. No subscription.

I bought the Full Unlock on another iPhone

Open Tools › Full Unlock › Restore purchase while signed in to the same Apple Account.

Can I use inches and feet?

Yes. Settings › Units › Imperial shows bending marks to the nearest 1/16" and runs in feet.
