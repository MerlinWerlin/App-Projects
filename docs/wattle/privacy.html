Wattle Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026

✓
Wattle collects no dataNo account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your jobs stay on your iPhone.

What stays on your iPhone

Calculations happen on your device. Saved jobs, notes and settings are stored in Wattle’s own app storage on your iPhone and are never uploaded. Wattle asks for no permissions: no location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos.

Sharing

Share job uses the iOS share sheet and sends the job’s text only to the app or person you choose.

Full Unlock and the 7-day trial

Apple processes the optional one-time Full Unlock and the free, user-started 7-day Trial. Wattle reads your App Store transaction records on your device to check access and the trial’s start date. It never receives your payment details. The trial date is also kept in the iOS Keychain so it can survive reinstalling. Nothing is charged automatically when the trial ends.

Updates and diagnostics

Wattle has no server of its own and sends no diagnostics. It does not download code: updates come only through the App Store.

Children

Wattle is a professional calculator for licensed electricians. It has no chat, social features or advertising.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its effective date if our practices change. Questions: open a support request.
